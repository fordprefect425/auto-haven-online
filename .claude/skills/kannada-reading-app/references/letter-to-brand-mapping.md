## Compound Characters (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ) — Advanced

Teach these only in Phase 4 (after all 49 base characters). The most common
compound characters to teach via real-world words:

| Compound | Word | Sign/Brand |
|----------|------|------------|
| ಕ್ಕ | ಅಕ್ಕ | Family store signboards |
| ತ್ತ | ಅತ್ತ | Direction signs |
| ನ್ನ | ಹಣ್ಣು | Fruit market boards (ಹಣ್ಣು ಅಂಗಡಿ) |
| ಲ್ಲ | ಹಲ್ಲು | Dental clinic signs (ಹಲ್ಲು ಕ್ಲಿನಿಕ್) |
| ರ್ | ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ | Karnataka name on all government boards |
| ಸ್ | ರಸ್ತೆ | Road signs |
| ಶ್ | ಮಿಶ್ರ | Mixed (ಮಿಶ್ರ ಅಂಗಡಿ) general stores |
