## Special Characters

| Character | Unicode | Name | Use |
|-----------|---------|------|-----|
| ಂ | U+0C82 | Anusvara | Nasal; used in ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು, ನಂದಿನಿ |
| ಃ | U+0C83 | Visarga | End-of-syllable h; rare in modern use |
| ್ | U+0CCD | Virama (Halant) | Removes inherent vowel; used in conjuncts |

## Font Recommendations

- **Noto Sans Kannada** — Google Fonts, free, full Unicode coverage
- **Kedage** — Traditional style, good for formal contexts
- **Tunga** — Microsoft's Kannada font, widely installed
- **Lohit Kannada** — Open source, good for app development

For the learning app: Use **Noto Sans Kannada** at 96sp+ for letter display.
